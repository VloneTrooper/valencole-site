Research posterSpring 2026AI + Ethics Workshop · University of Utah

Why Are People Miscalibrated on AI?

A poster with Teddy Wright on how identity-protective cognition distorts people’s views of AI, in both directions. When a person’s identity, livelihood or wellbeing is tied up in an old model of the world, updating gets costly, and they optimize for stability over accuracy without noticing. We were the only undergraduate presenters at the workshop.
